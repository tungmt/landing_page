## A small parking lot with big decisions

Don't Jam is my standalone 3D parking puzzle, built with Godot and GDScript for portrait mobile play. Clearing a path is only half the challenge: each vehicle must also be the right color for the passengers waiting outside the market.

Tap a vehicle with a clear exit to send it to a pickup bay. Matching passengers board automatically, and a full vehicle drives away to make room for the next ride. With limited bays, choosing the wrong vehicle can bring the whole pickup area to a standstill.

## Designed around thoughtful choices

- **Four vehicle types:** cars, vans, shuttles, and buses carry 4, 8, 12, or 16 passengers.
- **100 levels across 10 districts:** follow the journey from Meadow Village to Grand City, unlocking one market at a time.
- **Growing challenges:** later levels introduce larger boards, more vehicles, and more colors.
- **Earned-star powers:** use Undo, Extra Bay, or Shuffle to try a different approach.
- **A playful presentation:** toy-like vehicles, animated passengers, a winding journey map, and original music and sound effects.

## Building the game

The project brings together deterministic puzzle generation, collision and exit-path checks, a color-matched passenger queue, vehicle boarding and departure animations, and local progress persistence.

Each campaign level has a fixed layout and a verified-by-construction removal sequence. Automated tests cover the campaign solutions, pickup rules, star accounting, progression, and save recovery.

The interface adapts to portrait phone and tablet layouts, with large touch controls and a shared sound preference. Progress and earned stars are saved locally, without requiring an account.
