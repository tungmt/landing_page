# Privacy Policy for Don't Jam: Parking Puzzle

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

Tungmt ("we", "us", or "our") provides Don't Jam: Parking Puzzle (the "Game"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game and how you can contact us about privacy.

## 1. Scope

This policy describes the current version of the Game, in which advertising is disabled. The Game does not require an account or sign-in and does not offer in-app purchases.

Our website, Apple's services, and any services you choose to use outside the Game may handle information separately under their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores information locally to provide its features, including:

- Completed levels, best star ratings, and level progression.
- Your in-game star balance.
- Your sound preference.
- If present from an earlier version or session, records of earned advertising rewards, including transaction identifiers and reward dates or counts, to recover earned stars and prevent duplicate rewards.

The Game's own code does not upload these local save records to a server operated by us. We do not provide a cloud-save account or cross-device synchronization service.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings. Those backups are controlled by you and the platform provider, not by an in-game account.

## 3. Personal information and device permissions

The Game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, date of birth, or other account information. The current version does not display an age-entry screen.

The Game's features do not require access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo library.

We have not implemented our own analytics service, user profiling system, or cross-app tracking feature in the Game.

## 4. Advertising and third-party software

The Game includes Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform software through the Poing Studios AdMob integration. However, advertising is disabled in the current version: the Game does not initialize Mobile Ads through its advertising flow, request banner or rewarded ads, or display Google's advertising consent forms.

The presence of these libraries does not mean ads are currently displayed. We do not use the current Game to serve personalized ads or award stars for watching new ads.

For information about Google's privacy practices, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

If advertising or other data-handling features are introduced in a future release, we will update this policy and provide any notices, choices, or consent mechanisms required before enabling those features.

## 5. Support communications

If you contact us for support, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your contact details, a description of a problem, screenshots, or diagnostic details.

We use that information to respond to you, investigate problems, and provide support. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information that is not needed to explain your request.

## 6. Apple and operating-system services

Apple may process information in connection with App Store downloads, device backups, and diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's policies. If you choose to share app diagnostics with developers through Apple, Apple may make crash or diagnostic information available to us to help identify and fix problems.

These platform services are separate from the Game's local progress-saving features. You can review the relevant privacy and analytics controls in your device settings.

See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 7. How information is used and shared

Local game data is used to maintain your progress, balance, and preferences and, where applicable, recover previously earned rewards.

We do not sell your personal information or use your local game progress for targeted advertising. We do not operate a service that publishes your gameplay records or shares them with other players.

Information you send for support, or diagnostics made available to us, may be handled using service providers needed to receive and respond to requests. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, security, or safety.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed or replaced. On iOS and iPadOS, using **Delete App**, rather than **Offload App**, generally removes the app and its local data. Copies in device or cloud backups may remain until you remove them through the platform's backup controls, and restoring a backup may restore saved data.

Deleting the Game does not automatically delete support messages you previously sent us. We retain support communications only for as long as reasonably necessary to address the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations.

You can contact us to request deletion of support information associated with you, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Game may be used by a mixed audience, including children. The current version does not require an account, ask players to submit personal information, or display advertising.

Parents and guardians should supervise any support messages sent by a child and avoid including unnecessary personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and, where appropriate, delete it.

## 10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop using the Game and remove its local data by deleting the app. You can manage platform backups and diagnostic-sharing settings through your device or Apple account.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information we hold, or to raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

To make a request about support information or other information you believe we hold, contact us using the details below. Because the Game has no user account and local saves are not uploaded to our server, we generally cannot identify or retrieve a particular player's local save data remotely.

## 11. Security

We aim to limit information handling to what is needed to provide and support the Game. Device security, backups, and any information sent to us also depend on the systems used to store or transmit them. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Game or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.
